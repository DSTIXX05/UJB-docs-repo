# Product & Pricing Configuration

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image12.png)

Consider this module the Central Operations Hub of the UJB Mart infrastructure. Here, you control what services are sold, how much they cost, and how they behave on the client-facing application.

The hub is segmented into four core operational tabs:

## 1. Products

A Product represents a structured service package offered to consumers (e.g., Self Service, Weekly Regular, Executive Pak).

- Attributes: Every product requires a fixed Amount, a maximum Number of Items Covered, and the allowed Services Offered (e.g., Wash & Fold).
- Visibility Toggle: Products can be prepared in the background without being launched publicly. Until the "Featured" switch is toggled on, the package will remain invisible to users on the app.

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image13.png)

## 2. Items

Items denote the standalone clothing units or articles accepted for laundering (e.g., shirts, socks, dresses, bedding).

- Admins can easily append new clothing types and map corresponding graphic icons to them.
- Status Toggle: Deactivating or toggling off an item's status switch instantly hides that clothing category from the public-facing application.

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image14.png)

## 3. Services

Services outline the exact chemical and physical processing methods applied to garments (e.g., Wash and Iron, Dry Cleaning, Tie and Dye, Hand Wash).

- Admins have total liberty to define and introduce novel service treatments into the ecosystem.

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image15.png)

## 4. Delivery

This section houses custom logistics tiers and delivery methodologies.

- Admins can create distinct delivery modes, establishing specialized pricing adjustments and expected turnaround timelines (e.g., Express Delivery vs. Standard Delivery).

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image16.png)
