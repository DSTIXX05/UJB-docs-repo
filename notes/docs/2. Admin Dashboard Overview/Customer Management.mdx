# Customer Management

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image9.png)

The Customer Management ledger tracks user interaction data to assist in customer support and targeted business operations.

## Customer Directory Metrics

The primary directory focuses on key financial and demographic attributes, notably tracking the total Amount Spent per user—a strategic data point used to drive tailored business decisions, loyalty steps, and promotions.

## Customer Profiles

Clicking the 3-dot icon on a customer record opens a specialized detail overlay:

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image10.png)

- Account Identity: View metrics including Account Status (Active/Inactive), Total Orders placed, Email, Phone Number, and registration date.
- Administrative Actions:
  - **View Order History:** Instantly review a granular timeline of their past transactions.
  - **Send Message:** Send communications directly to the user.
  - **Block Account:** Permanently or temporarily freeze the user’s platform access in cases of policy violation.
