# Team Invitation & Role Delegation

To safely scale administration, the platform includes a secure user-onboarding protocol.
![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image22.png)

## Inviting a New Team Member

![User portal screenshot](/img/ss/image23.png)

1. Click the Invite Team action at the bottom-left of the main navigation menu.
2. In the modal overlay, enter the user's First Name, Last Name, Email, and Phone Number.
3. Click Send Invite. 📩 Onboarding Flow: Once sent, the platform triggers an invite to the recipient's email address, prompting them to log in securely and establish their personalized, private passwords.
